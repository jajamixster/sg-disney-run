Daily Meal Plan

Daily Macro Targets — 91.8 kg · 171 cm · Training Days

🟢 Protein ~180–185g / day (2.0g × 91.8 kg) · ~720 kcal
🔵 Carbohydrates ~250–275g / day (fuel for runs) · ~1060 kcal
🟠 Fats ~70–75g / day (hormones & recovery) · ~650 kcal

Total ~2,430 kcal on training days — moderate deficit for body composition improvement while fuelling gym sessions. Rest days: cut carbs by ~30% (~175g), keep protein the same.

🌅 Pre-Run / Pre-Gym (30–60 min before)

Light carbs + small protein. No heavy food before training.
2 slices white toast + peanut butter 30g C · 8g P
1 banana 27g C · fast energy
400ml water before gym hydration

🍳 Post-Gym Breakfast (within 45 min)

Hit 40–50g protein fast — this is the most important meal.
1 scoop whey protein in milk (300ml) 25–30g P · 15g C
3 whole eggs + 2 egg whites scrambled 25g P · 5g F
80g oats with berries 50g C · 4g P
5g creatine monohydrate (stir into oats) ★ supplement

🥗 Lunch (12:00–1:00 PM)

Largest meal of the day. Bangkok-friendly options listed.
200g grilled chicken breast / pla kapong / tofu 44–48g P
1 cup jasmine rice or sweet potato 50g C
Stir-fried greens / som tam (no sugar) fibre · micronutrients
½ avocado or 1 tbsp olive oil in dressing 10–15g F

🍌 Mid-Afternoon Snack (3:00–4:00 PM)

Keep protein coming in every 3–4 hrs to hit 180g daily.
Greek yoghurt (200g, plain) or Noosa 20g P · 10g C
Handful of almonds or mixed nuts (30g) 6g P · 16g F
1 apple or 2 mandarins 20g C · vitamins

🍜 Dinner (7:00–8:00 PM)

High protein, moderate carbs. Reduce rice on rest days.
200g salmon / lean beef / khao man gai chicken 44–48g P
Stir-fried vegetables (unlimited — pad pak) fibre · micronutrients
½ cup rice (skip on rest days) 30g C
1 tsp sesame oil or fish sauce dressing 5g F · flavour

🌙 Pre-Sleep Protein (9:30–10:00 PM)

Slow-digesting protein for overnight muscle repair. Closes the protein gap.
200g cottage cheese OR 24g P slow
1 scoop casein protein + 300ml low-fat milk 33g P slow
Daily protein total check: aim for 180g+ ★ key target

⚡ Creatine Protocol

Creatine monohydrate improves high-intensity performance, sprint finishes, and speeds recovery between hard sessions. It takes 3–4 weeks to fully saturate muscles — start in Week 2 for it to peak right before race day.

Loading Phase (Optional)

20g / day

Split into 4 × 5g doses for 5–7 days (Weeks 2). Saturates muscles faster. Can cause temporary water weight and bloating — manageable.

Maintenance Phase

5g / day

One serving every morning, mixed into your post-run breakfast shake or oats. Continue through race day and beyond.

No-Loading Alternative

5g / day

Skip loading — just take 5g daily from Week 1. Takes 3–4 weeks to saturate but no bloating. Either approach works for a 7-week plan.

Race Day Tip

5g + H₂O

Take your normal 5g dose the morning of July 5 with your pre-run breakfast. No special timing needed — it works at saturation level, not acutely.

Bangkok Gym Training Tips
🎚️

Treadmill Incline

Set incline to 1% on all easy runs — it better simulates outdoor road running and engages your glutes more. Only go 0% for stride sessions.

💧

Gym Hydration

AC gym air is dry — drink 500ml during each treadmill session. Bangkok heat outside means 3–3.5L total daily water. Electrolytes post-run.

🚣

Rowing Machine Benefit

At 91.8 kg, rowing is your best cross-training tool — it burns high calories with zero joint impact. Great for active recovery between run days.

🧂

Sodium & Creatine

Creatine pulls water into muscles — you need more sodium and fluids. Don't under-salt meals. Coconut water (no sugar) is a great natural electrolyte drink in Bangkok.

😴

Sleep (Non-Negotiable)

7–8 hours minimum. Sleep is when muscle protein synthesis peaks and creatine works. Race week: sleep by 10 PM Fri/Sat before the Jul 5 start.

⚖️

Weight at 91.8 kg

Joints take ~3× body weight impact per stride. The Novablast 5 is the right call for training — its cushioning is specifically important at your weight. Don't skip it for the Takumi Sen on long sessions.

🌊
Race Day — Singapore Tips
🌡️

Race Day Heat

Sentosa at 6:30 AM is already 28–30°C and humid with little shade. This is very different from your AC gym. Your body needs to adjust — factor in a slower pace vs treadmill.

💦

Pre-Race Hydration

Drink 500–600ml water 90 min before the start. Sip 200ml at the start line. Don't chug right before — you'll cramp. Start hydrating the night before.

🎽

Kit Collection

Collect race kit Jun 27–28 at Uniqlo Flagship, Orchard Central (11am–7pm). Test your full outfit on your Week 6 Wednesday run — nothing new on race day.

🏁

Pacing Strategy

Don't sprint km 1 — outdoor heat will punish you. Run km 1–3 comfortably, then push km 4–5. A positive split in the heat costs more than it gains.
